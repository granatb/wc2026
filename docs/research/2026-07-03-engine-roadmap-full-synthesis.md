# wc2026/evmax Engine-Improvement Roadmap

Synthesized from 11 research angles. Ranking = (impact × evidence strength) / effort. Pipeline stages referenced: **S1** odds fetch/cache → **S2** de-vig → **S3** lambda solve → **S4** scoreline model (DC) → **S5** 50k Monte Carlo → **S6** player event sim → **S7** fantasy scoring/outputs → **S8** research overlay → **S9** backtest harness.

---

## 1. Executive Summary — Best 5 by evidence-per-effort

1. **Shin/power de-vig replacing proportional (S2), applied hardest to AGS props.** Independently flagged by 5 of 11 researchers. Proportional normalization is measurably the worst mainstream method (Strumbelj 2014: Shin best in 217/412 bookmaker/sport pairs, bias cut ~⅔; Clarke et al. 2017: multiplicative "performed badly on all measures"; Buchdahl 136k matches: equal-margin worst). Payoff concentrates exactly where this engine lives: 20–50% overround goalscorer props and lopsided WC 1X2 — proportional de-vig deflates favourites/stars, matching the −2.3 xPts-low audit. ~50 lines stdlib, days of work.
2. **Lambda identification from Asian handicap + full totals ladder instead of 1X2+one total (S1/S3).** Plain-Poisson 1X2 inversion under-recovers ~0.10–0.15 goals/team (opisthokonta, measured), and 1X2 carries favourite-longshot bias while AH is statistically unbiased (Hegarty & Whelan, IJF 2025, 80k matches). This is the single most direct documented mechanism behind the audit gap.
3. **Decomposed minutes model: P(start)/P(cameo)/P(unused) × conditional minutes, drawn per sim (S6).** The consensus culprit for the low bias and the identified commercial differentiator (OpenFPL attributes FPL Review's remaining edge on zeros/blanks to xMins). Flat "starters = 60+" understates 85–90-min stars and zeroes supersubs.
4. **Measurement first: log-loss/CRPS harness scored against the de-vigged-odds baseline, with per-component bias decomposition (S9).** Whole model classes are separated by RPS ~0.002–0.013 — without proper scoring you cannot tell any of these changes from noise. Also settles whether the −2.3 gap is bias (fixable) or Rotowire miscalibration.
5. **Persist the 50k joint sims as a scenario matrix and make decisions on it (S7).** The engine already computes the joint distribution DFS papers had to approximate with copulas (Hunter-Vielma-Zaman; Haugh-Singal, Mgmt Sci 2021) — then throws it away. Persisting per-sim player-point vectors makes captain EV, P85, blowout coverage, portfolio and rank objectives exact column sums. Near-free.

## 2. Quick Wins (S/M — do within 2 weeks), ranked

1. **Shin + power de-vig (S2).** Implement `shin()` (fixed-point on insider fraction z; mberk/shin and R `implied` as references) and `power()` per market family: Shin for 1X2 and AGS, power for 2-way totals. Keep proportional as config fallback; A/B on cached R1–R3 odds vs the Rotowire gap.
2. **AGS prop inversion fix (S2→S6).** Three coupled corrections: (a) Shin/power de-vig of the whole AGS board; (b) invert p = 1−exp(−θ) and *deconvolve the book's embedded minutes assumption* (λ90 = θ/(E[min]/90)) so your own start probabilities re-apply; (c) reconcile the de-vigged booksum to sum(1−exp(−λᵢ)) implied by team lambda, after a ~3–4% own-goal haircut. Star rates rise, fringe longshots fall — the most fantasy-points-relevant single bias.
3. **Log-loss harness + component decomposition (S9).** Score 1X2/totals/CS/AGS/xPts with ignorance score (Wheatcroft: beats RPS/Brier at selecting the better forecaster) *always as delta vs the de-vigged odds baseline*; decompose realized-vs-projected points per component (minutes, goals, assists, CS, SoT, saves, DC-estimate) per position — realized components already exist in decoded stats_rN.json IDs. Add the FPL-Review-style "perfect model" noise ceiling by scoring the engine against its own sim-drawn synthetic outcomes.
4. **Scenario matrix export (S5/S7).** Shared sim index across a round's matches; write players × 50k int16 per round (~40MB). Immediately re-derive: per-sim captain EV (P85, P(best captain)) replacing 2×mean; leverage columns; Holdet pair values.
5. **Skellam/AH+totals-ladder lambda solve (S1+S3, M).** Extend the odds cache to keep AH line+price, every O/U line, BTTS. Solve S from AH via Skellam CDF inversion, T from the totals ladder (weighted by 1/margin), λ=(T±S)/2; demote 1X2 to fitting DC rho. With ≥4 constraints, free per-match dispersion/dependence; else pin tournament-level.
6. **Minutes mixture model (S6, M).** P(start) from beta-binomial on recency-weighted team sheets (seed: core/realized.py priors; overlay hard facts force 0/1); minutes|start and minutes|cameo from published tournament sub-timing distributions (mean sub 70.6', 4.26 subs/match, losing teams sub at 55.3'). Draw appearance state per sim; gate 60-min appearance/CS points per draw. Add team-level conservation check (Σ outfield minutes = 990+stoppage, one GK×90) as a bug detector.
7. **Extra-time correction for knockouts (S3+S5, M).** All inputs are 90-minute objects (AGS settles at 90'; lambdas are 90'). On drawn 90' sims, append an ET branch: +30' exposure at scaled intensity, extra sub, continued accrual, penalties from the P(advance)−P(win90) residual where a to-advance market exists. ~5–8% xPts uplift on balanced ties; matters for every remaining match.
8. **Joint scorer–assister draw (S6, S).** Per simulated goal: draw scorer, then assister conditioned on scorer (no self-assist, ~25–35% unassisted). Fixes joint outputs (stacks, Holdet pairs) at trivial cost.
9. **Parameter-uncertainty layering (S5, S).** Draw λ from Gamma mixing, start-probs from Beta, shares from Dirichlet before each sim (stdlib `random.gammavariate`). Point-parameter sims are provably overconfident; this honestly widens P85/ceilings.
10. **Per-match DC rho from BTTS (S3, M).** Solve rho by bisection against de-vigged BTTS per match; parameterize theta vs supremacy (McHale-Scarf: dependence goes negative with mismatch). Directly prices the Målspillet X-0 vs X-1 call and CS mass.
11. **Holdet kr-growth as a first-class sim output (S6/S7, S).** Map each sim's event vector through the kr table per-sim, emitting E[growth], P85 growth, growth covariance, captain-growth EV — Holdet's actual objective. Add exact cash flows (1% fee, 1% bank interest) to the trade rule.
12. **MOTM settlement forensics (S9, S).** Cross-match cached Holdet 513 winners against WhoScored/Sofascore/Opta-Points top-rated per match. If one provider matches ≥90%, MOTM becomes a deterministic event-linear function (like Betfair's Opta POTM) — prediction, not modeling. Highest information-per-effort in the awards domain.
13. **Confirmed-XI re-run mode (S6/S7, S).** `--confirmed` flag: snap p_start to 1/0 from team sheets, re-run only the player stage, log prior-vs-posterior deltas to price what news is worth.
14. **Structured news schema replacing hand-set w (S8, M).** Overlay entries become {status, source_class, timestamp, trust} → calibrated p_start mapping; per-source trust measured against realized DNPs (formalizes the SportsMole lesson). Hard facts stay absolute.
15. **Gamma-Poisson team-strength nudges with an Elo K=60 guardrail (S3, S).** Conjugate posteriors on opponent-adjusted xG, prior n₀≈15–30 matches (Ley: 3-year half-life for national teams ⇒ 3 games move strength only ~5–8%); weight →0 when fresh odds exist; clamp any strength move to K=60 Elo-equivalent. Codify the negatives: no rest-differential adjustment unless ≤3 days (Scoppa), no travel adjustment, altitude only for acclimatization asymmetries.

## 3. Structural Upgrades (L), ranked by impact

1. **Goal-time simulation (Dixon-Robinson birth process) with score-coupled substitution hazards (S5/S6).** Replace scoreline draws with timed goal arrivals (intensity rising through the match, score-state dependent — DR 1998, 4k+ matches), calibrated so the aggregate grid still matches the market. Attach piecewise exit hazards responding to simulated score (losing teams sub at 55.3' vs ~60'). One coherent change unlocks: correct minutes exposure (integral of λ(t) over on-pitch window — subs score 13.2% of goals on ~10% of minutes), real player–scoreline correlation (the blowout-coverage failure), CS credit for early-subbed players, ET extension, late-goal clustering. Prerequisite: quick wins 6–7 give partial value earlier.
2. **Player-level rate models replacing static goal-shares (offline fit → cached JSON, S6).** Two evidence-backed layers: hierarchical shot-generation × conversion priors with hard shrinkage of finishing (McHale-Szczepanski JRSS-A 2014; ASA: G−xG has ~zero year-over-year persistence), and/or FPL-Review-style GBM per-90 rates (their model beat bookmaker AGS implications, R² 0.353 vs 0.321; OpenFPL replicates commercially-competitive accuracy from free data). Fit offline in any stack, ship coefficient tables; reconcile to team lambdas as today. Fixes prop-less players — the Holdet cheap-boomer class. Requires the ToS-clean data layer (StatsBomb open data, public Wyscout, FBref/Understat via soccerdata) keyed by fifaId.
3. **Ownership model + field-simulation rank engine (new module consuming the scenario matrix).** Snapshot FIFA selection % / Holdet popularity per round; Dirichlet-multinomial field model (Haugh-Singal, Mgmt Sci 2021 — real-money validated); sample opponent squads, score them on the same 50k worlds, optimize P(top-x%) instead of E[points]. Fills the admitted "no ownership/rank game theory" hole; formalizes the contrarian-YOLO build and captain leverage. Ship the S-difficulty lite version first (xPts + λ·σ − γ·EO-overlap columns).
4. **Outright-odds ability inversion (Leitner-Zeileis, upstream of S3).** De-vig tournament-winner odds, invert via bracket simulation to per-team abilities → lambdas for future hypothetical pairings before match odds exist. Re-fetching per round harvests the market's own Bayesian updating — the R32 wildcard planner's missing input. Pairs with the MILP transfer planner (heuristic solver + optional HiGHS backend) and advancement-weighted horizons.
5. **Defensive-contribution simulation (S6, M/L).** Negative-binomial CBIT/CBIRT counts modulated by possession proxy (lambda ratio) and simulated game state; threshold points are P(X≥T), dispersion-sensitive — a mean-based role estimate is structurally wrong near the cutoff. Replaces the "role-shaped estimate"; prerequisite for the FPL port. Same machinery feeds in-sim BPS bonus for FPL.
6. **Pooled cross-competition validation corpus + point-in-time store (S9, L).** football-data.co.uk archive (20+ seasons, open+close odds) replayed through the full match layer for statistical power; append-only timestamped snapshots with dated overlay facts (current research files not being round-scoped is a live leakage bug). This is what makes w, tau, and de-vig choices *tunable* rather than hand-set.
7. **Weibull-count/CMP marginals + copula (S4, M/L).** Boshnakov et al. 2017: beat DC in/out of sample with positive Kelly returns. Do CMP (20 lines, 90% of the benefit) *or* Weibull-copula, not both; identify shape from the totals ladder once #5-quick-win lands. Value is CS/ceiling/correct-score mass, not 1X2 — rank below the items above.

## 4. Moonshots (XL) worth tracking

- **Large Events Model (LEM).** Autoregressive event-sequence simulators (Mendes-Neves et al., Machine Learning 2024; 62.2% next-event accuracy, public code) conditioned to odds-implied lambdas, pre-generated offline → cached rollouts. Highest ceiling (natively correlated everything), floor near zero: no published fantasy-points win, player conditioning is open research. Gate on the harness existing.
- **Hybrid covariate residual corrector (Groll et al.).** RF/ridge on log(realized intensity) − log(odds-implied λ) trained on WC 2002–2022 — the only published class to beat WC bookmaker odds out of sample (leave-one-tournament-out), but n≈64/tournament and stdlib tree ensembles hurt; freeze coefficients from an offline sklearn fit.
- **Belief-state MDP / approximate-DP planner (Matthews-Ramchurn, AAAI 2012: ~top percentile of 2.5M managers).** Value function V(bank, team value, rounds left) by backward induction over bracket continuations. Marginal over a decayed-horizon MILP on a 4-round knockout is unproven; bigger payoff for the 38-GW FPL season.

## 5. Measurement Prerequisites (build into S9 before judging anything)

- **Match probabilities (1X2, totals, CS, BTTS):** ignorance/log-loss primary, RPS+Brier secondary, *always as skill delta vs the de-vigged closing-odds baseline* (raw scores are near-tautological since lambdas are solved from the same book).
- **Goal-count distributions:** non-randomized PIT histograms (Czado-Gneiting-Held) per round; reliability diagrams for CS/over-2.5/BTTS.
- **Player event probabilities (AGS, CS, assist, 2+ goals):** beta calibration curves (Kull et al. — parametric, small-sample-safe; isotonic overfits <1000 obs); walk-forward refits only.
- **Player points distributions:** sample-based CRPS from the 50k sims; pinball loss at τ=0.85 to validate the P85 claim; per-position mean-error (bias) — signed, not absolute — per component.
- **Noise ceiling:** score engine means against sim-drawn synthetic outcomes (FPL Review "perfect model": RMSE ~2.7–2.9 is unbeatable) to split the −2.3 gap into bias vs irreducible variance.
- **Fast feedback:** CLV loop — second odds snapshot at kickoff−1h; measure whether the overlay moves probabilities toward or away from the close (direct test of w and lineup-news quality).
- **Joint calibration:** predicted vs realized *pair-event* frequencies (stack double-returns, GK+DEF CS combos) vs an independence baseline — the only direct test of correlation work.
- **Decision metrics:** top-K capture, captain regret, lineup regret vs ex-post optimal, Målspillet pick log-loss; rank metrics (Spearman per position) sidestep level bias.
- **Honesty kit:** paired permutation/block-bootstrap tests, HLN-corrected Diebold-Mariano, minimum-detectable-effect statements; cluster bootstrap by match at player level. 104 matches resolve only large effects — pool player-matches (~2,700/tournament) and the historical corpus.
- **Awards/prices:** MOTM top-1 hit-rate vs goal-favorite and rating-provider baselines (promote only after beating baseline two consecutive rounds); Brier on price rise/fall predictions.
