## Captaincy

- **Haaland (vs Bournemouth H) is the majority call** — FFS, RotoWire, FPL Copilot's squad piece. Historical case: 37 goals in his first-six-game windows across four City seasons.
- **Bruno Fernandes (at Hull) is a large minority** — AllAboutFPL and SI captain him; FPL Copilot's model actually projects him top for GW1 (6.7 xPts). Best FPL player since Carrick took over, record 24 assists last season.
- Differential armband mentioned twice: **Gabriel** (set pieces vs Coventry).
- Net: either premium is defensible; nobody recommends captaining outside those two except as a stated punt.

## Squad structure consensus

- 3-5-2 / 3-4-3 with **two field premiums (Haaland + Bruno = £27.5m)** is the default; a minority add a third premium (Palmer/Gabriel) or drop Haaland for midfield saturation (explicit "pros/cons" archetypes at AllAboutFPL).
- Double budget GK (£4.5m x2, rotating fixtures) widely preferred over one £6.0m keeper (SI, RotoWire); FFS still like Raya.
- Cheap defence: £4.0-5.5m starters from good-fixture teams; £4.0m promoted-team defenders (Diop, Thomas, Greaves, O'Shea) as enablers.
- Money concentrated in midfield; third forward kept at £6.0m or below.
- Bench should *play* (early-injury insurance), not be pure fodder.

## GW1 vs whole-season framing

- Universal tone: **don't overthink GW1; avoid early rank losses by holding the template core** — differentials are for later.
- The distinctive 2026/27 GW1 factor is the **summer 2026 World Cup hangover**: late-returning internationals lack pre-season and are minutes risks. RotoWire's avoid-list is built on it (Guehi, Rogers, Rice, Cunha, plus O'Reilly role-change and Gyokeres rotation); FFS flag the same for Arsenal's deep-run players.
- **Chips**: sets unchanged (2x WC/FH/TC/BB, first set expires GW19). Live debate is an early Bench Boost — GW1 (full playing bench, then strip bench value at the wildcard) vs GW2 (built around Coventry (H) vs Hull) — vs holding everything. Common wildcard plan: **~GW6**, once five games of real data exist.

## Rule changes 2026/27 vs 2025/26 (all sources agree)

1. **Live ranks/leagues**: points, projected bonus (from ~20 min into each match), overall rank, mini-leagues, H2H and Cup all update live in-app.
2. **BPS rework** (to de-overlap with DefCon): tackled-deduction removed (helps dribblers); CBI 1 BPS per 3 actions instead of per 2 (nerfs dominant CBs); GK saves restructured — 2 BPS base, +1 in-box, +1 big-chance save (Fix reads net penalty-save at 7 BPS; FFS state 8 unchanged — minor source conflict).
3. **Gameweek lockdown** now 09:00 UK the day after the final fixture.
4. **Unchanged**: chips, 5-transfer rollover, DefCon scoring, deadlines.
5. **No AFCON December transfer bonus** (tournament moved to June/July 2027).
6. Cosmetic: percentile ranks, career averages, badges, recaps.
7. Price context: premiums repriced upward — Haaland £15.5m is "the most expensive player in FPL history" (AllAboutFPL); budget £100m unchanged.
