1. **Haaland** ~73%, £15.5m — "comfortably the most-owned player in the game"
2. **Bruno Fernandes** ~50%
3. **Joao Pedro** ~50%
4. **Szoboszlai** 35%+

Structure read: the attack is settled (consensus on premiums), the defence is fluid — Raya top GK at under 30%, top defenders bunched at 24-26%, i.e. no agreed back line yet.

Caveats they stress: pre-deadline ownership is polluted by auto-picked squads; injuries/transfers can still reshape the template (they cite a hypothetical Vinicius-to-Arsenal as the kind of move that would); the real template only crystallizes at the GW1 deadline.

## Rule changes 2026/27

- **Live everything**: points, overall ranks, mini-leagues, H2H and FPL Cup ties update in real time during matches — kills the need for third-party live-rank tools (LiveFPL's core use case now native).
- **BPS**:
  - Tackled-deduction removed → benefits high-volume dribblers.
  - CBI: 1 BPS per 3 actions (was per 2) → trims centre-back bonus, reduces DefCon double-dipping.
  - GK saves: 2 BPS base, +1 for in-box saves, +1 for big-chance saves; net penalty-save reward down from 8 to 7 BPS.
  - Their read: favors busy GKs, attacking full-backs and creative players; slightly nerfs dominant CBs.
- **Chips/transfers unchanged**: 8 chips total in two sets, first set expires GW19; up to 5 free transfers roll over; no AFCON bonus transfers in December (tournament moved).
- **DefCon points unchanged** from 2025/26.
- Misc: gameweek lockdown at 09:00 UK next day; career-average percentile ranks; gameweek recaps and team badges return.
