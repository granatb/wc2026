## Rule changes 2026/27 (vs 2025/26)

- **Live updates**: projected bonus points added to scores after ~20 minutes of each fixture; overall ranks and mini-league standings now update live during matches.
- **BPS changes** (to reduce overlap with defensive-contribution points):
  - "Tackled" deduction (-1 BPS when successfully tackled) removed — helps dribblers.
  - Clearances/blocks/interceptions: 1 BPS per 3 actions (was per 2) — slightly worse for dominant CBs.
  - GK saves restructured: saves in box 3 BPS, outside-box save category removed, other saves 2 BPS, +1 BPS for big-chance saves; penalty saves 8 BPS.
- **Gameweek lockdown** now finalized 9am UK the day after the last match (was ~1h after final whistle).
- **Unchanged**: chips (two sets of WC/FH/TC/BB, first set dies at GW19 deadline), max five rolled free transfers, DefCon points system.
- **New UI**: season history with percentage rank, career averages, per-GW percentage rank.
- No AFCON bonus transfers this season (tournament moved to June/July 2027).
