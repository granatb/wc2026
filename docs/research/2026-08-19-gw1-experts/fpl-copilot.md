## Squad-construction philosophy

- Build around **one premium** (Haaland £15.5m, captain, non-negotiable) rather than spreading across several big names: "One elite premium plus nailed enablers beats four big names and a broken bench."
- Stack the midfield around him; value defenders at ~£5.0m who actually start; £4.0-4.5m bench enablers.
- Draft order: lock captain first → 1-2 premium mids → fill with value → prioritize fixture difficulty over price → watch friendlies until the 21 Aug deadline.
- Vice-captain ladder: Bruno Fernandes, Palmer, Saka depending on fixture.

## GW1 captaincy

- Model output: **Bruno Fernandes** top projected (6.7 xPts) for GW1 itself; Haaland the co-obvious alternative — the site's framing is that either obvious pick is fine.
- Differential armband: **Gabriel £8.0m** (set-piece goal threat + clean-sheet upside vs Coventry).

## Differentials and structure tips

- Sub-12%-owned mid-price midfielders projecting premium-level returns; Anderson and Tavernier (£6.0-6.5m, nailed minutes) named.
- Winning GW1 shape: Haaland + one premium mid for captain rotation; £6.0-6.5m nailed value mids; budget defence drawn from good-opening-fixture teams; a functional (playing) bench as early-injury insurance rather than pure fodder.
- Explicit advice: don't overthink the opener; clean-sheet odds should drive the defensive picks.
