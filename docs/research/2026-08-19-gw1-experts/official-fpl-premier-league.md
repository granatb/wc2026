Best: **Man Utd, Man City, Everton, Liverpool**.
- Liverpool: Newcastle, Forest, Ipswich, Fulham in the first four — van Dijk (£6.5m), Szoboszlai (£7.0m), Victor Munoz (£6.5m) flagged.
- Arsenal: only one top-six side in the first eight — Gabriel (209 pts last season) and Raya (£6.0m) flagged.

Worst: **Bournemouth** (three 4+ difficulty games early: Man City, Liverpool, Chelsea), **Coventry** (Arsenal and Man City away in first three), **Hull** (Man Utd, Villa, Chelsea, Newcastle in first five).

## Rule changes (official announcement)

- BPS reworked to cut overlap with defensive-contribution points: tackled-deduction removed; CBI 1 BPS per 3; flat save value with extra for close-range and big-chance saves.
- Projected bonus added live after 20 minutes; live ranks and mini-league standings.
- Chips unchanged: two sets (WC/FH/TC/BB), first set must be used before the GW19 deadline.
