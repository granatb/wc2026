## What remains unproven

Neither the corrected engine nor the ridge model has demonstrated a prospective
edge over market or external forecasts. Corrected player-only early-ridge heldout
RMSE is 1.990 versus 2.143 for last-four mean on 26,135 rows. The earlier evaluation
included Assistant Manager entries; original reports remain with a correction note.
This differs from the earlier six-week
research population and does not validate cold starts or new availability gates.
The market model still approximates BPS residuals, minutes marginals, cameos,
unknown-player allocation and several event types. Historical scoring differs.

Cohort membership is frozen before outcomes. Paired comparisons weight gameweeks
equally. Three-week moving-block intervals require 12 consecutive eligible weeks
and unchanged model versions; these choices are operational, not a power analysis.
Intervals remain exploratory and unadjusted for multiple comparisons. They do not
select a winner or prove that an approach will work next season.
