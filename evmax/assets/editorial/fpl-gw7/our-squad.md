## How confident are we?

We have more confidence in Saka as the first shortlist move than in the exact three-transfer bundle. The shortlisted portfolios retain positive gains in the tested minutes scenarios, but those scenarios are not calibrated probabilities. We cannot assign a defensible percentage chance that these are the right moves. Actual finances, late injuries, rotation and future transfers can change the ranking.
