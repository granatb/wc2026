# Holdet "YOLO-D" — 2nd gold team (anti-chalk variance build)

**Same rules and scoring as GOLD T1** (see `holdet_gold/rules.md` and the shared
table in `games/holdet_common.py`): Guld / unlimited contracts, 11 players no bench,
1% interest, 1% transfer fee, trade rule = delta > 1% × incoming price, captain ×2
free, budget 50M, max 4/nation, same position quirks, locks at first kickoff.

## What differs: the objective
This is the **variance / anti-chalk** team. Rather than maximise expected growth, it
maximises **rank ceiling**: deliberately fade highly-owned chalk and overweight
low-owned, high-ceiling differentials whose upside (not mean) justifies the slot.

- Prefer players with high goal/assist *variance* and low expected ownership.
- Accept lower mean growth for materially higher top-percentile outcomes.
- Captain choice may favour the higher-ceiling option over the higher-mean one.

## Order book
Same shape as GOLD T1 but flagged for variance: differential picks highlighted,
captain chosen on ceiling, trades evaluated on rank-upside as well as the trade rule.
